Concept C: Compact window with a version picker

  • The launcher shrinks to a small, phone-sized window: one big status, the two Open buttons, the version, and Start / Restart / Stop. Everything else (access, logs, settings) is one click away behind links and the gear.
  • The current UA version is a button. It always says what is newer, and it opens a picker sheet with the last five releases (right). Pick one, then Download only or Download and switch.
  • The launcher's own update appears as a strip under the title bar instead of a floating badge, because a small window has no room for one. The floating badge (bottom right) is shown for comparison.
  • Fits a menu-bar or tray app well and is the least intimidating. Weakest for troubleshooting, since logs and access move off the main view.
