Concept D: Update center drawer

  • The home screen stays about running your team. Everything about versions lives in one Updates drawer, opened from the header button (with a count) or from the floating badge.
  • Each update is a short checklist, so you always see the next step: Found → Download → Review notes → Switch for UA, and Found → Download → Restart launcher for the launcher. A step that is waiting on you has a button; a step in progress shows its percentage.
  • Other versions at the bottom of the UA lane lists the last five releases for rollback or pinning.
  • Clear for people who update rarely. The drawer hides versions until opened, which is less discoverable than concept A or B.
