Concept B: Sidebar with a Versions page

  • The launcher becomes a small app with sections: Home (status and Open), Versions, Access, Logs, Settings. This page shows Versions.
  • One page owns every update: the launcher card (0.5.0 → 0.5.5) and the UA table of the last five releases, plus pre-releases when the box is ticked. The sidebar's count tells you something is waiting.
  • Status, Start / Restart / Stop and Open Web Chat stay in the sidebar on every page, so lifecycle controls never move around.
  • Best if the launcher keeps growing (more settings, more diagnostics). Costs one extra click to reach anything that is not on Home.
