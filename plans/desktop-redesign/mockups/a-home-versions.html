Concept A: Home with a version list

  • Closest to today's layout. The UA updates panel becomes Choose which UA runs: the last five releases, always visible, each with its own action. Nothing depends on a notification you might miss.
  • Each row says whether it is checked for this launcher and whether it is already downloaded. Download never restarts UA; Switch opens a confirmation with the notes (try Switch back… on 0.9.1).
  • The header is one aligned row: a single Start / Restart / Stop group (the duplicate lower controls are gone), the status pill and the theme switch at the same height. Progress runs as a thin bar under the header.
  • Access, logs and settings shrink to summary cards that open their full view, so the home screen fits without scrolling.
  • The launcher's own update is the floating badge at the bottom right. Use the mockup bar to see its three states.
